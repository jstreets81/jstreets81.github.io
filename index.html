    About

    A Madrid-based creator turning real people's stories into AI-crafted songs, mixing techno, classical, pop, rock and more into meaningful soundtrack memories.

    Every Jonny Streets track starts with a person, a place or a feeling. The words and the vision come from real stories; the sound is shaped with AI. Songs in English and Spanish, from the chess-board concept album Your Move to singles like Qué Cielo Te Cuidará.
